# Preflight: The Ninth Furnace

**Verdict:** ADAPT. Chapter 1 has a strong hook and the novel pays off its promise in chapter 6. The chapters in between would lose listeners as they stand. Those problems are in how the chapters are told, not in the story, so the adaptation plan can fix them.

**The promise from chapter 1:** The boy the whole sect laughed at has a hungry furnace inside him, and he is going to make every one of them kneel.

## Predicted drop-off

These are predictions from the text, not measured retention.

| Episodes | Watch | Risk as drafted | Cause |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | %H1 | low | none. It opens on the failure and ends on the furnace's promise |
| 2-5 | %H5 | high | exposition dump, name flood, recaps, hero missing, payoff told in one line, three endings at rest |
| 6-10 | %H10 | not assessed | the sample has only six chapters |

## Why

1. **Payoff drought**, chapters 2 to 5. Chapter 1 promises revenge. The first win arrives in chapter 5, and it is summarised instead of played: "he passed the assessment easily and was ranked fortieth. A few people were surprised." A listener who stayed for that moment gets one sentence. Of the causes here, this one would cost the most listeners.

2. **Exposition dump**, chapter 2. "The first is Qi Condensation, which has nine stages. After Qi Condensation comes Foundation Establishment, which has three stages..." The script flagged 22 new names and terms in 180 words. It follows straight on from the strongest line in the novel, so episode 2 would open by asking the listener to memorise a list.

3. **Hero missing**, chapter 4. Elder Mo and Elder Zhang drink tea and talk sect politics, and Li Wen never appears. Drafted as-is, this chapter sits in episode 3.

4. **Closed endings**, chapters 2, 3, 4, and 5. "By morning he felt a little stronger." "He ate in the dining hall, then went back to his room and went to sleep." "Sipping his tea and looking out at the mountains." "Satisfied with his progress." The draft split ends episode 2 on chapter 3 and episode 3 on chapter 5, which means two of those lines would end episodes.

5. **Name flood and recap bloat**, chapters 3 and 5. Chapter 3 introduces Steward Hu, Elder Fang, Senior Brother Qiao, Qiao Lin, Disciple Bai, and Elder Mo in one paragraph. Chapters 3 and 5 both open by retelling earlier events, starting "As we know".

## Episode plan

| Episode | Source | Ends on | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ch 1 | "Feed me, and I will make every one of them kneel." | none |
| 2 | ch 2, ch 3 | Senior Brother Qiao watching Li Wen from the steps | Cut the realm list to one realm, played during the breathing scene. Cut the recap. Keep two names, Steward Hu and Senior Brother Qiao; Elder Fang, Qiao Lin, and Disciple Bai become "the elder", "his cousin", and "a disciple". End on Qiao, not on sleep. |
| 3 | ch 5, then ch 4 | Elder Mo deciding he wants the boy | Play the assessment as a full scene. Cut the recap. Reorder so the politics come after the win, cut to Elder Mo's one reason to care: he needs disciples before the next assessment. |
| 4 | ch 6 | "Say yes. Let them all watch." | none |

The episode count stays at four, but the hero is in every episode and every episode now ends on an open question.

## Rewrites

### Episode 2, opening

The voice did not let him sleep.

"Your sect calls it Qi Condensation," the ninth furnace said. "The first realm. There are nine of them. Tonight, this is the only one you need."

Li Wen sat on the woodshed floor with his back to the wall.

"Nine Furnace Breathing. Eighty-one cycles. You do not stop."

He breathed in. The cold cracks inside him ached, then burned. Somewhere past the thirtieth cycle he stopped being sure he was breathing at all.

"And after tonight," the furnace said, "you will need spirit stones. Many of them."

### Episode 2, ending

By evening his hands were green with herb sap, and every muscle ached from the night before.

He looked up only once.

On the steps above the gardens stood Senior Brother Qiao, the strongest disciple in the outer sect. He was watching Li Wen, and he did not look away.

### Episode 3, opening

A month after the whole sect laughed at him, Li Wen stood in line for the monthly assessment.

The same faces. Some of them still grinned when they saw him.

His turn came. He stepped forward.

"Fortieth," the examiner said.

The grinning stopped.

### Episode 3, ending

High on the inner peaks, Elder Mo set down his tea.

Fortieth. A month ago that boy could not light a testing stone.

The Grand Elder's seat would go to whoever held the most disciples before the next assessment. Elder Mo had just found one he did not understand.

## After launch

- **%H1** tests the hook. If it comes in weak, the problem is the premise or the voice, not the plan, because episode 1 was not changed.
- **%H5** tests the plan. This is where the draft was predicted to lose the most listeners. Compare it with shows on the same slate that hold well through episode 5. If this show still falls hard by episode 5, the diagnosis was wrong, and the next place to look is the voice and the pace of the performance.
