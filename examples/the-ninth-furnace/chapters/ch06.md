# Chapter 6 — Su Yan Returns

The gates of the outer sect opened, and Su Yan walked through them on the arm of Senior Brother Qiao.

Every head in the courtyard turned toward Li Wen.

"I heard the cripple is still here," Qiao said loudly. "Sweeping leaves, is it?"

Su Yan finally looked at him. "Leave, Li Wen. You are embarrassing both of us."

Qiao drew his sword. "Or stay, and I'll show her what you really are. Duel me. Tomorrow. In front of everyone."

The courtyard went silent.

Deep inside Li Wen, the ninth furnace stirred and laughed.

"Say yes," it whispered. "Let them all watch."
