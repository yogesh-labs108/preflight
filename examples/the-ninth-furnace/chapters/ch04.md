# Chapter 4 — Elder Mo's Plan

In the inner peaks, Elder Mo poured tea for his guest, Elder Zhang of the Discipline Hall.

"The Grand Elder grows old," Elder Mo said. "When he retires, the seat will go to whoever controls the most core disciples."

Elder Zhang sipped his tea. "The Su family supports you. And the Qiao family supports me. Neither of us has enough alone."

They talked for a long time about the balance between the peaks, the coming Sect Tournament, and which disciples could be recruited before the next assessment.

When Elder Zhang left, Elder Mo sat alone for a while, sipping his tea and looking out at the mountains.
