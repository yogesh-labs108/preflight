# Chapter 5 — The Monthly Assessment

As we know, Li Wen was thrown out of the inner sect after failing the testing stone, and has been working in the herb gardens of the outer sect while secretly cultivating with the help of the ninth furnace.

A month passed. It was time for the monthly assessment.

Li Wen stood in line with the other outer disciples. When his turn came, he passed the assessment easily and was ranked fortieth. A few people were surprised.

That evening he returned to the herb gardens, satisfied with his progress.
