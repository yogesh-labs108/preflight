# Chapter 2 — The Nine Realms

On the Azure Cloud Continent, cultivation is divided into nine great realms. The first is Qi Condensation, which has nine stages. After Qi Condensation comes Foundation Establishment, which has three stages: early, middle, and late. Then Core Formation, then Nascent Soul, then Spirit Severing, then Void Refinement, then Tribulation Crossing, then Mahayana, and finally Immortal Ascension, which no one has reached in ten thousand years.

The Azure Cloud Sect itself is divided into the outer sect, the inner sect, and the core disciples. Above them are the elders, who are divided into outer elders, inner elders, and the Grand Elder. The Sect Master rules over all of them.

Spirit stones come in low grade, middle grade, high grade, and supreme grade. One middle grade stone is worth one hundred low grade stones.

The voice in Li Wen's furnace explained that to begin, he would need to absorb the energy of spirit stones slowly, through the Nine Furnace Breathing Method, which has eighty-one cycles.

Li Wen practised through the night. It was difficult, but by morning he felt a little stronger.
