# Chapter 1 — The Cracked Furnace

The testing stone stayed dark.

Four hundred disciples of the Azure Cloud Sect watched sixteen-year-old Li Wen press his palm to it for the third time. Nothing. Not a flicker.

"Meridians crippled," the examiner announced. "Dismissed from the inner sect."

Someone laughed. Then everyone did.

Across the courtyard, Su Yan stepped forward. Two years they had been engaged. She did not look at him. Her father held up the betrothal token and snapped it in half.

"The Su family does not marry cripples."

That night Li Wen sat alone in the woodshed they had given him, pressing his hands against his stomach where his cultivation should have been. Where there should have been warmth, there was only a cold, cracked emptiness.

Then the emptiness spoke.

"Eight furnaces burned before you, boy. Eight emperors. Every one of them was called a cripple once."

Li Wen froze. "Who's there?"

"I am the ninth furnace. And I am very, very hungry."

Something inside him cracked open, and it was not pain.

"Feed me," the voice said, "and I will make every one of them kneel."
