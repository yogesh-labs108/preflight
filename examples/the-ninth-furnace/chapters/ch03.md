# Chapter 3 — The Outer Sect

As we know, three days ago Li Wen failed the testing stone in front of the whole sect. His engagement to Su Yan was broken by her father. Then, that same night, a voice spoke to him from the cracked furnace inside his body, calling itself the ninth furnace. Since then he has been practising the Nine Furnace Breathing Method.

Now Li Wen reported to the outer sect.

Steward Hu gave him a grey robe and a room at the end of the eastern row. Elder Fang, who managed outer sect duties, assigned him to the herb gardens. Senior Brother Qiao, the strongest outer disciple, watched him from the steps with his cousin Qiao Lin. Disciple Bai showed him where to collect his rations. Elder Mo passed by without a word.

Li Wen worked in the herb gardens until evening. He ate in the dining hall, then went back to his room and went to sleep.
